The precinct

Built in clusters. Serviced like a town.

The village is organised in clusters of 120 modular units, each with its own mess and kitchen, laundry, lounges and courts. Clusters share a town centre with a community hall, supermarket and shops, sports oval, pool and water tower. The services precinct sits south, on its own freight road.

    • Units with individual climate control, gas hot water and standby power

    • Long-term lease or campaign-based agreements

    Why not a closed camp

    Closed workforce camps are cheap to run and expensive for the region: money leaves on the same plane as the crew. Dunmarra is built so the services a workforce needs also serve the people who already live here, and so a share of the roles are filled locally. For operators that means a social licence case that stands up in front of Traditional Owners, the NT Government and the public.

    Community

    Open to the region

    Dunmarra is not a permanent town and not a closed camp. It is a serviced village where the grocery, clinic, pool, gym and venues are run commercially and open to anyone, including the people of Elliott, Daly Waters and surrounding communities.

    Services you can use

    Grocery, medical, fitness, pool and licensed venues operate as businesses, priced for the public. Nothing is free, and nothing is behind a boom gate.

    Jobs that stay

    Hospitality, maintenance, retail, transport and site services are local roles first. Training pathways are being developed with regional providers and Traditional Owner organisations.

    Territory first

    The precinct is designed so a Beetaloo workforce can rotate from Darwin and Alice Springs rather than interstate or overseas, keeping wages and spend in the Territory.

    Project

    Staged to demand

    Dunmarra Development Land Pty Ltd is delivering the precinct in stages that match Beetaloo activity, with each stage a complete, operable village.

    Stage 1100

    First cluster, mess and laundry, services precinct stage 1 lots, town water and power.

    Stage 2500

    Four clusters, town centre core: supermarket, hall, clinic, first venues.

    Stage 31,500

    Oval, pool, school and clinic land, airstrip terminal, expanded freight precinct.

    Stage 43,000

    Full build-out across eight villages with reserved commercial expansion land.
