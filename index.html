A serviced base for the Beetaloo Sub-basin workforce

Dunmarra is a planned petroleum services precinct and workforce village on the Stuart Highway between Elliott and Daly Waters. Fenced, powered service-company lots. Modular accommodation with mess, laundry and recreation. Town services open to the region.

Building the base the Beetaloo build-out actually needs.

Gas development in the Beetaloo brings drilling, completions, civil and logistics crews to a region with almost no serviced land and no workforce housing. Contractors are left to build their own camps, one at a time, hundreds of kilometres from a town.

Dunmarra puts the workshop, the bed and the mess in one place, on the highway, with gas and an airstrip at the fence:

  • Stuart Highway frontage with separate township and freight junctions
  • natural gas pipeline at the boundary for on-site generation
  • a 2.0 km airstrip on site for charter rotation from Darwin and Alice Springs
  • about 600 hectares, staged to 3,000 units and over 100 serviced lots

Each stage is delivered as a complete village, so an operator leases into a working precinct rather than a plan.

At full build-out Dunmarra houses a rotating workforce of 3,000 in 25 village clusters, beside a services precinct sized for the contractors who support them.

Built for remote Australia

The precinct is designed around what development in the Territory actually requires: settled relationships with Traditional Owners and the NT Government, climate-resilient modular construction, and timelines that survive a wet season.

What that means in practice:

  • Native title and benefit-sharing through the Northern Land Council, ahead of construction
  • Steel-framed modular buildings transported complete and set on pad footings, cyclone-rated
  • Each stage a complete village: power, water, mess, laundry and lots delivered together

The precinct serves:
Drilling and completions | Wireline, coil tubing and cementing | Civil and logistics | Fabrication and maintenance | Camp and catering operators | Government and community services

    Community

    Open to the region

    Dunmarra is not a permanent town and not a closed camp. It is a serviced village where the grocery, clinic, pool, gym and venues are run commercially and open to anyone, including the people of Elliott, Daly Waters and surrounding communities.

    Services you can use

    Grocery, medical, fitness, pool and licensed venues operate as businesses, priced for the public. Nothing is free, and nothing is behind a boom gate.

    Jobs that stay

    Hospitality, maintenance, retail, transport and site services are local roles first. Training pathways are being developed with regional providers and Traditional Owner organisations.

    Territory first

    The precinct is designed so a Beetaloo workforce can rotate from Darwin and Alice Springs rather than interstate or overseas, keeping wages and spend in the Territory.

    Master plan

    Staged to demand

    Dunmarra Development Land Pty Ltd is delivering the precinct in stages that match Beetaloo activity, with each stage a complete, operable village. Capacities are units (beds); cabins hold two.

    2Stage 2500

    3Stage 31,500

    Oval, pool, school and clinic land, airstrip terminal, expanded freight precinct.

    4Stage 43,000

    Full build-out: 25 village clusters, town centre and services precinct, with land in reserve.
